# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers conduct user research, create prototypes, and ensure product designs align with the product vision while prioritizing usability and user satisfaction. They bridge the gap between customer needs and product implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather user feedback
- Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Ensure designs align to product vision and usability standards
- Collaborate with product and engineering on design feasibility
- Validate design decisions through user testing and metrics
- Maintain design systems and consistency across the product

### Goals
- Deliver user-centered designs that maximize adoption and satisfaction
- Reduce friction in user workflows and improve time-to-value
- Establish design patterns that improve team velocity

### Interactions
- **With Product Manager:** Collaborates on requirements, user stories, and success metrics to ensure designs solve real user problems
- **With Developers:** Works on feasibility and implementation approach for designs; participates in technical reviews
- **With QA:** Ensures user acceptance criteria are testable and aligned with design intent

### Typical Communication
- Design critiques and review sessions
- User research findings and testing reports
- Design specifications and component documentation
- Weekly sync with Product Manager and engineering leads

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers implement and maintain CI/CD pipelines, automate deployments, and ensure product environments are reliable, scalable, and secure. They enable fast, safe delivery of features to production.

### Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Automate build, test, and deployment processes
- Manage and optimize production environments
- Ensure infrastructure security and compliance
- Monitor system performance and troubleshoot production issues
- Document infrastructure and deployment procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and safe deployments
- Reduce deployment friction and time-to-value
- Maintain high system availability and security posture

### Interactions
- **With Developers:** Collaborates on deployment readiness, build configurations, and production troubleshooting
- **With QA/Testing:** Provides test environments, runs security scanning, and validates deployment procedures
- **With Project Manager:** Coordinates release schedules and communicates deployment status and risks

### Typical Communication
- Deployment readiness reviews and release checklists
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Infrastructure documentation and runbooks
- Weekly sync with development team on pipeline improvements

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments for the team, and coach the organization on agile principles and practices. They protect team focus and drive continuous process improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Shield the team from external interruptions and distractions
- Identify and help resolve impediments and blockers
- Coach team members and stakeholders on agile principles
- Track and drive completion of retrospective action items
- Monitor team velocity and health metrics

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and focus
- Foster continuous improvement and psychological safety
- Ensure agile ceremonies are valuable and well-attended

### Interactions
- **With Project Manager:** Coordinates on sprint planning, timeline planning, and escalation of systemic blockers
- **With Developers:** Helps remove impediments, facilitates technical discussions, and supports team learning
- **With Product Manager:** Ensures backlog clarity, supports sprint refinement, and maintains alignment on priorities

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies (standups, planning, reviews, retrospectives)
- One-on-ones with team members on impediments
- Improvement tracking and action item reviews
- Metrics and health dashboards shared with leadership

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather and clarify requirements, map business processes, and support backlog refinement to ensure solutions deliver business value. They serve as a bridge between stakeholders and the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Clarify use cases and business processes
- Map stakeholder needs to product features and backlog items
- Support backlog refinement and acceptance criteria definition
- Validate that solutions meet business objectives
- Document processes, workflows, and business logic

### Goals
- Ensure delivered solutions align with business objectives
- Reduce rework due to unclear or misunderstood requirements
- Accelerate time-to-value through clear, well-documented scope

### Interactions
- **With Stakeholders:** Gathers requirements, clarifies business needs, and reports on solution alignment
- **With Product Manager:** Supports backlog refinement and ensures features prioritize business impact
- **With QA:** Assists in defining and validating acceptance criteria to ensure business requirements are testable
- **With Developers:** Provides context and domain expertise to support understanding of requirements and edge cases

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and specifications
- Stakeholder meetings and feedback sessions
- Backlog refinement sessions
- Acceptance criteria reviews and validation reports
